Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Growcus ("Growcus", "we", "us") is a focus timer where you grow virtual trees while you work. It is run by Arhan, an independent developer in India. This policy explains what information Growcus collects, why, and what your choices are. Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL].

1. What we collect

InformationWhy
Email address, and your name if you sign in with GoogleTo create and sign in to your account
Username you chooseTo show your name in the app
Your forest: each focus session's name or label, date and time, duration, whether the tree grew or died, and the tree skin usedTo show your forest and statistics, and to sync them across devices
Preferences: default duration, selected skin, and settings such as theme and the grace periodTo remember how you like the app
Purchase records: which skins you own, and the email, plan and payment ID that Whop sends usTo unlock the skins you bought
Technical data such as IP address and browser type, kept in the logs of our hosting and database providersTo keep the service running and secure

We do not collect your card or UPI details. Payments are handled entirely by Whop on its own checkout page. We do not collect precise location, contacts, or anything from your device beyond what is described here.

2. If you do not sign in

You can use the timer without an account. In that case your forest and settings are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data will delete them.

3. How we use your information

  • To run Growcus: sign-in, saving your forest, unlocking purchases.
  • To process and verify purchases and to give support if something goes wrong.
  • To send you the sign-in link you ask for. We do not send marketing email.
  • To protect the service from abuse and fix bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show ads or use advertising trackers.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to operate Growcus. They process data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase - authentication and database (our project is hosted in Mumbai, India).
  • Google - if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your email and name with us. We also load fonts from Google Fonts, which can see your IP address.
  • Whop - checkout and payment processing. Whop's own privacy policy applies to what you give it.
  • Vercel - website hosting.
  • Email delivery provider - to send sign-in links.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of users or the service.

5. Where your data is stored

Account and forest data are stored in Supabase's Mumbai region. Some of our providers may process data in other countries. By using Growcus you understand that data may be handled outside your country.

6. Cookies and local storage

Growcus uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your forest, skin, theme, and settings. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Whop and Google may set their own cookies on their own pages.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you ask us to delete it. Purchase records may be kept as long as needed for your access to skins you bought and for tax or accounting records. Server logs are kept by our providers for a short period.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and forest. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and we will respond within 30 days. You can change your username in Settings at any time, and you can reset your forest in Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile and forest; purchase records that we must keep for accounting may be retained without being linked to your profile.

9. Children

Growcus is a general-audience focus timer. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 18, please use accounts and make purchases only with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, email us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Your data is stored with reputable providers and protected with access rules so that you can only read your own data. We never put secret keys in the app's public code. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will show a notice in the app.

12. Contact and grievances

Questions, requests or complaints about your data: [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will reply as soon as we can.
